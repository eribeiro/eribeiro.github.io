I'm a software engineer with over 25 years of experience, mostly in backend services, databases, and cloud infrastructure. Today I work on multi-tenant  open-source SaaS for Brazilian's legislative systems. Before that I worked at Amazon and DataStax, and contributed to various open source projects like Cassandra, ZooKeeper, and Solr, among others. I've also taught graduate courses on databases and distributed databases at the University of Brasília. I like connecting theory with practice and writing about what I learn.

Lately I've been looking at local AI agents, LLM architectures, and AI-assisted software engineering (skills, agents, orchestration), along with decision models, AI software factories, and the agentic web. I want to see how these tools help with everyday engineering without giving up reliability, privacy, or human judgment.

For now I'm mostly exploring on my own, building prototypes and trying practical uses of AI at work and in personal projects.
